## How to use this file

Each "Slide" below maps to one deck slide. "Visual" describes the layout using
design-system tokens. "Notes" is the speaker note. Keep one idea per slide.

---

### Slide 1 - Title
Headline: NarrativeOS
Subhead: The operating system for product storytelling.
Footer: Powered by StoryGTM.
Visual: bg-primary (#0a0a0f), wordmark centered, accent-gradient underline.
Notes: Set the tone - calm, premium, confident. One breath, then advance.

### Slide 2 - The Problem
Headline: Every team tells a different story.
Body: Product says one thing, marketing another, sales a third. Buyers hear
noise, not a narrative - and deals stall.
Visual: three misaligned speech bubbles in text-secondary; one turns error red.
Notes: Make the pain concrete. Name the cost: lost deals, slow launches.

### Slide 3 - Why It Matters Now
Headline: Complexity is rising faster than clarity.
Body: More products, more channels, more competitors. Without one spine, every
new asset adds drift.
Visual: rising line chart in accent-primary vs a flat "clarity" line in muted.
Notes: Tie to the audience's current reality; keep it 20 seconds.

### Slide 4 - The Idea
Headline: Treat storytelling like an operating system.
Body: One narrative core. Consistent inputs. Repeatable, on-brand outputs.
Visual: central gradient node with modules orbiting it.
Notes: This is the "aha". Slow down here.

### Slide 5 - What NarrativeOS Is
Headline: One core, many assets.
Body: Product Understanding, Positioning, Story Architecture, Creative Hooks,
Battle Cards, Launch Planning - all drawing from the same source of truth.
Visual: module grid using bg-card tiles with accent icons.
Notes: Breadth without clutter - point, don't read every tile.

### Slide 6 - The Master Narrative
Headline: The spine every asset traces back to.
Body: Challenge -> Solution -> Transformation. Stated once, reused everywhere.
Visual: three-act horizontal bar; each act in a semantic tint.
Notes: This is the backbone referenced across the whole brand system.

### Slide 7 - Proof / Assets
Headline: From narrative to ready-to-use assets.
Body: A saved battle card, an objection guide, a launch pitch - generated,
reviewed, and stored in the Asset Library.
Visual: three exported-asset cards in the export style.
Notes: Show, don't tell. If live, demo the Asset Library here.

### Slide 8 - How It Stays On-Brand
Headline: Governance built in.
Body: Standards, review, and an Asset Library as the system of record keep every
output consistent as teams scale.
Visual: simple lifecycle chevrons Draft -> Approved -> Published.
Notes: Reassure the buyer this does not create chaos - it removes it.

### Slide 9 - The Transformation
Headline: One voice. Faster launches. More wins.
Body: Teams operate with clarity, speed, and confidence.
Visual: the misaligned bubbles from Slide 2, now aligned in accent-gradient.
Notes: Callback to Slide 2 - the payoff lands emotionally.

### Slide 10 - Call to Action
Headline: Build your narrative core.
Body: Start with one product story and let every asset flow from it.
Footer: NarrativeOS - Powered by StoryGTM.
Visual: single primary gradient button "Launch Dashboard".
Notes: End on one clear next step. Stop talking after the ask.
