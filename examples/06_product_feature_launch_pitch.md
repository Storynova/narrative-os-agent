## Launch Snapshot

Feature: NarrativeOS Asset Library - one-click save & reuse for every story asset.
Launch type: Major Feature. Phase: General Availability. Timeline: Q3 2026.
Success metrics: 40% of active users save >=1 asset in month 1; 25% reuse an asset
within 30 days; +15% weekly active usage.
Priority channels: Website, Email, Social.

---

### Slide 1 - Title
Headline: Introducing the Asset Library.
Subhead: Save any story asset once. Reuse it everywhere.
Visual: gradient title on bg-primary; small "New in NarrativeOS" pill.
Notes: Name the feature and the benefit in one breath.

### Slide 2 - The Pain Today
Headline: Great assets, then lost forever.
Body: Teams generate a sharp battle card or launch story, paste it into one doc,
and never find it again. Every request starts from scratch.
Visual: scattered document icons fading to muted.
Notes: Get a nod of recognition before pitching the fix.

### Slide 3 - The Feature
Headline: One click to save. One place to find.
Body: Every generated asset can be saved to the Asset Library with a type, title,
and date - then viewed or reused anytime.
Visual: Asset Library card grid using bg-card + accent border.
Notes: This is the core demo moment.

### Slide 4 - How It Works
Body (3 steps):
1. Generate an asset in any module (battle card, story, launch plan).
2. Save it - it lands in the Asset Library with a type and timestamp.
3. Reuse - View to reopen, or drop it into a deck or enablement tool.
Visual: three numbered chips in accent-primary.
Notes: Keep it to three steps; simplicity is the selling point.

### Slide 5 - Launch Timeline
| Window | Focus |

Visual: horizontal timeline with semantic checkpoints.
Notes: Shows the launch is planned, not improvised.

### Slide 6 - Channel Messaging
Website: "Introducing the Asset Library - save & reuse every story asset."
Email: "[New] Never rebuild a battle card from scratch again."
Social: "We built the Asset Library because great assets deserve a second life."
Visual: three channel tiles, each with the tuned line.
Notes: One consistent idea, adapted per channel - proof the brand system works.

### Slide 7 - Success Metrics
Headline: What good looks like.
Body: 40% save >=1 asset (month 1). 25% reuse within 30 days. +15% weekly active
usage. Reviewed at the end of each launch window.
Visual: three metric cards; targets in success green.
Notes: Commit to measurement; it builds credibility.

### Slide 8 - Call to Action
Headline: Stop rebuilding. Start reusing.
Body: Save your first asset today and watch your library compound.
Footer: NarrativeOS - Powered by StoryGTM.
Visual: single gradient CTA button.
Notes: End on one clear next step.
