# Objection-Handling Guide — StoryNova (vs. LinearDeck context)

> ILLUSTRATIVE Phase 1 sample from the Objection agent. In production these entries are mined from real CRM + call-intelligence data; frequencies below are placeholders.

Each entry uses a Feel–Felt–Found structure and attaches a proof point.

---

### Objection 1: "You're a newer brand with fewer templates than LinearDeck."
**Frequency:** high (placeholder)
**Response:** I understand — template breadth feels like the safe choice. Other enablement leaders felt the same, until they found that template count wasn't the thing losing deals; inconsistent messaging was. StoryNova imports your existing brand kit and keeps every asset on-message from one spine.
**Proof point:** One canonical spine powers every asset (internal://architecture/README#core-idea).

---

### Objection 2: "We'd need to connect our CRM and call tools to get value."
**Frequency:** medium (placeholder)
**Response:** That's a fair concern. Teams have felt that integrations mean a slow start. What they found is StoryNova works standalone on day one — connectors simply make objection handling sharper over time by pulling in your real closed-lost reasons.
**Proof point:** Objections grounded in real call + CRM data (crm://salesforce/closed-lost?window=90d).

---

### Objection 3: "How do we trust the competitive claims the AI generates?"
**Frequency:** high (placeholder)
**Response:** Exactly the right question. Buyers have felt burned by tools that hallucinate competitive facts. What they found with StoryNova is that the Critic agent blocks any claim without a verifiable source before it ever reaches an asset.
**Proof point:** Every competitive claim is citation-gated (internal://agents/orchestrator.py#evidence-gate).

---

### Objection 4: "Our reps already go off-message; a tool won't fix behavior."
**Frequency:** medium (placeholder)
**Response:** Agreed that tools alone don't change habits. Teams felt that too — then found that giving reps a single, always-current source of truth removed the guesswork that caused drift in the first place.
**Proof point:** One canonical spine powers every asset (internal://architecture/README#core-idea).
